About CareerSetu India

Your trusted Indian platform for discovering jobs, internships and career opportunities.

What is CareerSetu India?

CareerSetu India is a student-focused career platform designed for Indian students, freshers and professionals who want to find genuine jobs, internships and career resources in one place.

We connect Indian candidates with employers through job listings, internships, fresher opportunities and practical career resources. Candidates can search by city, category, skills and employment type, while employers can discover relevant Indian talent.

Our goal is to support job seekers across India, including Pune, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Delhi NCR, Ahmedabad, Kolkata and other growing hiring hubs.

Our Mission in India

Make job searching in India simple, transparent and accessible for everyone!

Every person deserves an opportunity that matches their skills and ambitions. CareerSetu brings useful Indian job opportunities together in one simple place, so candidates can spend less time searching across multiple websites.

For employers, we simplify hiring by connecting companies with relevant Indian candidates through a modern and easy-to-use platform.

We Are Here to Help

Need help? Our team is here to support you with profile creation, job applications, internships and interview preparation.

We focus on practical career support and help you find opportunities that match your skills, education and career goals.

For employers, we encourage genuine job listings and a professional hiring experience for candidates across India.

Our Team CareerSetu

CareerSetu India is a student-built web project created to make the Indian job search experience easier for students, freshers and professionals.

Aarav Sharma, Priya Deshmukh and Rohan Kulkarni are part of the student development team. The team works together on design, frontend development, job data, usability and the overall candidate experience.

CareerSetu is also a practical learning project where web development skills are used to solve a real-world problem: helping people discover jobs, internships and career resources in India.

Our focus is on the needs of Indian students and freshers looking for internships and their first jobs. That perspective guides the features and improvements we make to the platform.

Together, we are building CareerSetu into a useful Indian career platform, with a focus on simple job discovery, fresher opportunities and practical career guidance.
